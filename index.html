The Middleman Crisis vs. The DirectFarm Solution

Why eliminating intermediaries changes everything for Indian agriculture and health

Traditional Middleman Mandi System

  • 3 to 5 Intermediaries: Produce passes through commission agents (Aadhati), brokers, and traders before reaching the city.
  • Farmers Lose 40-50% Profit: Middlemen extract massive commission fees, transport margins, and forced distress cuts.
  • Delayed Payments: Farmers often wait weeks or months for their money or get trapped in credit cycles.
  • Consumers Pay Inflated Prices: You pay 2x to 3x more for vegetables and fruits that sat in godowns for days.

DirectFarm Direct Connection

  • Zero Intermediaries: Consumers buy straight from verified local farmers with complete transparency.
  • 100% Profit to the Farmer: Farmers set their own direct prices and keep 100% of every rupee earned.
  • Direct Instant Payment: Consumers pay farmers directly via Cash or direct UPI upon harvest delivery.
  • Harvest-Fresh Quality: Produce is harvested same-day or fresh from the farm, tasting authentic and pure.

About DirectFarm

DirectFarm was born out of a crucial realization: despite working grueling hours under the scorching sun, our farmers receive only a small fraction of the retail food price due to multiple layers of middleman interference.

By leveraging technology to build a direct bridge, DirectFarm eliminates unnecessary brokers and commission cuts. Farmers gain financial independence, fair livelihood, and pride in their craft, while consumers receive pure, chemical-free food directly from the hands that grew it.
